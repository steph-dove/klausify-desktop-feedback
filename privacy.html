Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026 · Draft pending legal review.

1. What Klaussy collects

Klaussy is a desktop application. It does not host a backend service of its own. Information you interact with from within Klaussy goes to third parties whose policies govern what they collect:

  • Your AI coding agent. When you use the terminal, the Plan / Debug / Review actions, "Explain diff", inline AI edit, or AI commit-message generation, the agent you select reads the prompt and repository context you provide and sends it to that agent's provider — Anthropic (Claude Code), OpenAI (Codex), Google (Gemini), or GitHub (Copilot). See the relevant provider's privacy policy.
  • GitHub. When you view / comment on / merge PRs or run CI checks through Klaussy, those requests go through your local gh CLI to GitHub. See GitHub's privacy policy.
  • Ollama (optional). If you enable inline AI completion, Klaussy runs qwen2.5-coder:1.5b locally via Ollama. No data leaves your machine.

2. What Klaussy stores locally

  • Your project list, terminal sessions, notes, and preferences, in ~/Library/Application Support/Klaussy/config.json.
  • Logs of main-process activity (errors and diagnostics), in ~/Library/Logs/Klaussy/main.log. No prompt contents are logged.

None of this leaves your machine unless you explicitly sync your home directory to iCloud, Dropbox, etc.

3. What Klaussy sends off-device

  • Update check. Klaussy checks whether a newer version exists so it can update itself. That request reveals your IP address and the version you are running. It carries no personal data, no identifier we assign you, and nothing about your projects.

That is the only request Klaussy makes on its own behalf. There is no license check, no activation, no account, and no server of ours that receives your data.

Everything else that leaves your machine is a request you triggered: the coding agent you selected, or a CLI such as gh that Klaussy runs for you. Those go to that provider, not to us, and are covered in section 1.

4. Telemetry

Klaussy does not send usage analytics or telemetry. We do not track which features you use, what projects you open, or how often you launch the app.

5. Third-party services

Klaussy orchestrates several tools you choose to use. Their policies apply:

  • Anthropic (Claude Code)
  • OpenAI (Codex)
  • Google (Gemini)
  • GitHub (incl. Copilot)
  • Ollama (local, no data sent off-device)

6. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Delete your local data by removing ~/Library/Application Support/Klaussy and ~/Library/Logs/Klaussy.

7. Contact

Open an issue in the feedback repo for any privacy-related question.

8. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page and bump the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the release notes of the version that introduces them.
